UNDERSTANDING VA WORK DIRECTIONS

Look beyond the title. Look at the work.

You’ve already learned the foundations of working as a VA.

Now we’ll look at something different: the range of work that VAs can actually do.

You may have seen titles like Virtual Assistant, E-commerce VA, Customer Support VA, Social Media VA, Amazon VA and many others.

But a title only gives you part of the picture.

In this module, you’ll look at real kinds of VA work, notice the patterns behind them, and collect your own clues about what you’d like to explore.

Your direction is not a permanent decision. It’s a direction worth exploring next.

LET’S LOOK AT THE WORK

Your Work Clues will stay with you as you continue through Stage 4.

Section 1 · Look Behind the Job Post

A VA job title can tell you very little.

You’ve already encountered different VA directions in the Beginner VA Journey.

Now let’s look closer.

Imagine you see these job posts online. Before thinking about whether you would apply, look at what the person would actually be doing.

The question is simple:

What kind of work is hiding behind the title?

Activity

Same Title, Different Work

Discovery

What did you notice?

You may have seen a pattern:

  • One title can contain several kinds of work.
  • Different titles can contain similar kinds of work.
  • Some titles describe the business area or system more than the work itself.

Key idea

The title is a clue. The task list tells you much more.

You’ll use that idea throughout this module.

Section 2 · What Kind of Work Is This?

Let’s give the work a language.

You don’t need to memorize a list of VA “types.”

Instead, we’ll use five simple work patterns to describe what people actually do.

A real VA role can contain more than one pattern.

These patterns are not personality types. They are simply a way to look at the work.

Pattern reference

The five work patterns

Remember

These are work patterns, not labels for people.

A single VA role can involve several of them.

Section 4 · Build My Work Clues

What caught your attention?

You’ve now looked at different kinds of VA work.

Some may sound interesting. Some may leave you unsure. Some may make you think, “That’s probably not for me.”

All three reactions are useful.

There is no score here.

You’re simply collecting clues to help you explore intelligently in the next parts of Stage 4.
