## 1. Purpose

4.4 is the last part of Stage 4:

**4.1 WORK → 4.2 ME → 4.3 MATCH → 4.4 DIRECTION**

| Module | Question |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | What is the work? |
| 4.2 | What have I actually done? |
| 4.3 | Where do those two views connect? |
| **4.4** | **What am I going to explore next?** |

By now the learner has noticed Work Clues (4.1), recorded real tasks with honest evidence levels (4.2), and examined directions with their own judgments, reasons, related tasks, what may be new, and questions (4.3).

4.4 does **two new things only**:
1. it **assembles** what the learner already recorded into one map whose directions they choose; and
2. it asks for **one concrete next exploration step** per direction, or one investigation step if they are still exploring.

Everything else on the map comes from earlier modules.

---

## 2. Core Design Principles

### Assemble, don't re-ask.
If 4.1–4.3 already hold it, 4.4 shows it and links to where it can be edited. 4.4 owns only: which directions are on the map, the next steps, "still exploring" and its investigation step, and "where I am now".

### The map is current thinking, not a decision for life.
Every view of the map, on screen and in print, says so.

### The learner decides what goes on the map.
No direction is pre-added, suggested, ranked, grouped by judgment or highlighted.

### Both outcomes are complete.
One or two directions, or "Right now, I'm still exploring", are equally complete outcomes. Neither is presented as better, in copy, order, size or visual weight.

### Choosing a direction is not readiness.
Nothing in 4.4 says or implies the learner is ready for, suited to or qualified for a direction.

---

## 3. Boundaries

### 4.4 may:
- show the learner's own records from 4.1–4.3, read-only, with links back;
- let the learner put up to two directions on their map, or choose to keep exploring;
- ask for one concrete next exploration step per direction, or one investigation step;
- ask an optional, narrow "where I am now" question;
- produce a readable, printable map;
- point forward to PRACTICE & PROVE as the place where the work itself is tried.

### 4.4 must not:
- score, rank, weight, compare-to-recommend, or calculate similarity;
- pre-add or suggest a direction, including from 4.3 choices;
- ask again for reasons, related tasks, "what may be new" or learning questions;
- ask the learner to rate their abilities or readiness;
- write to, change or delete any 4.1, 4.2 or 4.3 record;
- set practice tasks, build a portfolio, or assess work (Stage 5);
- cover resumes, cover letters, job-platform profiles, applications, proposals, interviews, pricing or client positioning (ENTER THE MARKET);
- require paid tools, sign-ups or contacting anyone;
- create a long-term career plan;
- award a certificate or badge, or route to a portfolio submission.

## 4. What 4.4 reads and what it owns

| Source | Where it appears in 4.4 | Who can change it |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 Work Clues | Count on Screen 1, with a link. On the map: the learner's own 4.1 reaction to each map direction, if they saved one (§12) | 4.1 only |
| 4.2 experiences and tasks | Count on Screen 1, with a link. On the map: only the tasks linked in 4.3 (D14), each with its recorded evidence answer | 4.2 only |
| 4.3 reflection per direction (judgment, reason or "no reason yet", related tasks, what may be new, learning questions) | Judgment on Screen 2; full reflection on the map | 4.3 only |
| 4.3 choice (selected directions, or undecided, plus the undecided note) | Screen 2, labelled "What you chose in Module 4.3"; undecided note on a still-exploring map | 4.3 only |
| **Directions on the map (0–2)** | Screens 2–4 | **4.4** |
| **Next exploration step per direction** | Screens 3–4 | **4.4** |
| **"Still exploring" and its investigation step** | Screens 2–4 | **4.4** |
| **"Where I am now" (optional)** | Screens 1 and 4 | **4.4** |

### Current records, current map
- The map **reads the current 4.1–4.3 records each time it is shown**. It never stores a copy of them.
- If the learner edits a 4.3 reflection (or a 4.2 task, or a 4.1 reaction) after saving the map, the on-screen map shows the updated content.
- Those edits never change the map's **own** content: which directions are on it, the next steps, "still exploring" and its step, and "where I am now" stay exactly as the learner left them.
- The learner can print an updated copy at any time. **Copies already printed or saved as PDF do not change.**

### Application facts relevant here
- 4.3 reflections can be edited but not deleted: `PVAS4.m43` in `public/shared/state.js` has no delete for explorations.
- A 4.3 reflection counts as saved when it has a judgment and a saved time (`isSavedExploration`, `public/shared/state.js`). The state layer accepts clearing a judgment (`setJudgment(dirId, null)`), which would make a saved reflection count as unsaved. Whether the 4.3 page lets a learner do that **[requires source verification]**. §13.5 defines what the map does either way.
- References to deleted 4.2 tasks are dropped from 4.3 reflections automatically (`normalize43`, `public/shared/state.js`), so the map never shows a task that no longer exists.
- A learner's 4.1 reaction to a direction is a Work Clue with source type `direction` and the direction's id (`getClue`, `public/shared/state.js`). It is keyed by the same id, so showing it with that direction involves no matching.

## 6. Learner Flow — four screens

### Screen 1 — Look back

**Purpose:** a short reminder of what the learner has done and where to find it. Counts and links only (D18).

**Shows:**
- Work Clues saved in 4.1 — link to 4.1;
- experiences and tasks recorded in 4.2 — link to 4.2;
- directions with a saved reflection in 4.3, and what they chose there — link to 4.3.

**DRAFT copy:**
> "Here's what you've worked through so far. You don't need to repeat any of it. In this module, you'll choose what goes on your map and decide on one next step."

**Then:** the optional **"Where I am now"** field (§10).

**Action:** Continue.

**Empty states:** zero counts are shown plainly ("No Work Clues saved yet") with no judgment. Nothing on this screen blocks continuing.

---

### Screen 2 — Choose what to explore

**Purpose:** the learner deliberately decides what goes on their map.

**Shows:**
1. **What you chose in Module 4.3** — the directions selected there, or "You chose to keep exploring", or "You didn't make a choice in Module 4.3". Reference only; nothing is added to the map from it (D2).
2. **Directions you've examined** — every direction with a saved 4.3 reflection, in the locked Direction Card order (01–11, `PVA_M41.DIRECTIONS`, `public/shared/m41-data.js`). Each shows the learner's own 4.3 judgment as a plain label, in the live 4.3 wording: "Explore further", "Not sure yet" or "Not for me right now" (`public/shared/m43.js`). Not grouped, sorted, coloured or highlighted by judgment.
3. **Add to my map** on each, up to two. Available whatever the judgment, with no warning (D17).
4. **Right now, I'm still exploring** — an equal option, with the same visual weight as adding a direction.
5. A link: **Examine another direction in Module 4.3**.

The map is in exactly one of three states (§13.1): empty, directions (1–2), or still exploring. Directions and "still exploring" are mutually exclusive, as in 4.3.

**DRAFT copy:**
> "Which directions do you want to explore next? Add one or two to your map—or keep exploring for now. Both are complete answers."

**Empty state — no saved 4.3 reflections:** no directions can be added. Show: "You haven't saved a reflection on any direction in Module 4.3 yet. You can examine one now, or keep exploring." Both the 4.3 link and "still exploring" stay available. Never a dead end.

**Action:** Continue (available once at least one direction is on the map or "still exploring" is chosen).

---

### Screen 3 — Decide on your next step

**Purpose:** turn each choice into one concrete action (§8).

**For each direction on the map:**
- the direction name, and the learner's own 4.3 learning questions if any (read-only, as prompts);
- one field: **My next exploration step**;
- the example ideas (§8), each with a **Use this idea** button.

**If still exploring:** one field, **My next step to help me decide**, with the investigation ideas (§7), each with **Use this idea**.

**DRAFT copy:**
> "Pick one small step you can do in the next few days, for free, without signing up for anything or contacting a client."

**Action:** Continue (available once every direction on the map has a step, or the investigation step is written). The learner can always go back to Screen 2.

---

### Screen 4 — Review and save my map

**Purpose:** see the whole map, save it, print it.

**Shows** the map exactly as it will print (§12), built from the current records.

**Actions:**
- **Save my Direction Map** — completes 4.4 the first time (§11);
- **Print my map**;
- edit links: change directions (Screen 2), change steps (Screen 3), edit "where I am now" (Screen 1), edit a reflection (4.3), edit tasks (4.2), edit a reaction (4.1);
- forward link to PRACTICE & PROVE, shown as "Coming later" until Stage 5 exists (§14).

**DRAFT copy (always visible, on screen and in print):**
> "This map shows where my thinking is right now. It is not a permanent career decision. I can change it at any time."

## 7. The Undecided Pathway

A learner who cannot choose yet:

1. sees their 4.3 record on Screen 2, including their 4.3 undecided note, if any, labelled as from 4.3;
2. chooses **Right now, I'm still exploring**;
3. writes one investigation step on Screen 3. Ideas offered (each with **Use this idea**):
   - Examine one more direction in Module 4.3. *(Always available; linked.)*
   - Read the Direction Cards for two directions and note how the daily work differs.
   - Read a few real job posts for two directions and note which tasks repeat in each.
4. saves on Screen 4. The map reads **"Right now, I'm still exploring"**, shows the directions they have looked at so far (§12), and their step.

This completes 4.4 (§11). Nothing on the path needs 4.2 tasks, Work Clues or a completed 4.3.

"Still exploring" in 4.4 is independent of "undecided" in 4.3 (D15). A learner undecided in 4.3 may add a direction in 4.4, and a learner who chose directions in 4.3 may keep exploring in 4.4. Neither module changes the other.

If the learner later adds a direction, their investigation step is kept, not lost (§13.3).

## 8. The Next Exploration Step

### What counts
A step is one action the learner can:
- do within a few days;
- do for free, without paid tools or sign-ups;
- do without a client, an application or a practice assignment;
- describe in a sentence.

### Example ideas for a direction (each with **Use this idea**)
- Read three real job posts for this direction and write down the tasks that appear more than once.
- Pick one task from the Direction Card that I haven't done and find a free explanation of how it's done.
- Find out what one tool named on the Direction Card is used for.
- *(Optional — shown as optional in the copy)* Ask someone who does this kind of work one question about their usual day. Never required.

Investigation ideas for "still exploring" are in §7.

### Use this idea
- It is pressed by the learner. Nothing is copied unless they press it.
- It copies that example's text into the step field and moves focus there, so the learner can edit it.
- If the field is empty, the text is copied.
- If the field already has text, the learner is asked first. **DRAFT copy:** "Replace what you wrote with this idea?" Cancel leaves their text unchanged. Their text is never overwritten silently.
- It does not save the step by itself, does not press Continue, and does not complete the module. The learner can ignore every idea and write their own text.
- **[requires specification]** 4.3 stores its text fields as the learner types (BUILD_REPORT_4.3 §5, interpretation 1). The specification must define when copied text becomes the learner's saved step — for example, only after the learner edits it or presses Continue — so that pressing the button alone never saves it.

### Not a next step (excluded from all examples and copy)
Applying for jobs; creating or updating a job-platform profile; writing a resume or cover letter; buying a course or tool; starting a practice project or portfolio piece; contacting potential clients.

### Vague steps
The application does not judge the learner's wording. The prompt and examples steer toward concrete steps; "learn more about it" is discouraged by the copy, not blocked.

### Not in v1
No "When I'll do it" field (D23).

## 11. Completion

4.4 is complete when the learner presses **Save my Direction Map** after:
1. reaching Screen 4; and
2. either
   - putting at least one direction on the map, each with a written next step; or
   - choosing "still exploring" with a written investigation step.

**Not required:** completing 4.1–4.3; "where I am now"; any Work Clues; any 4.2 tasks or linked tasks; two directions.

Completion is kept once set (Stage 4 convention: `completedAt`, as in 4.1–4.3). The map stays editable. If a later edit leaves a direction without a step, the map shows "No next step written yet"; if it leaves the map Empty, the map shows "Nothing on your map yet" with a link to Screen 2. Completion is not removed in either case.

**Hub (D24, D25).** The hub keeps its existing rule (`public/shared/home.js`): the Continue button goes to the earliest module that is in progress or not started. Once 4.1, 4.2, 4.3 and 4.4 are all complete, it reads **Review my Direction Map**. Completing 4.4 is valid whatever the state of 4.1–4.3; while an earlier module is unfinished, the map stays reachable from the hub's module list (where 4.4 shows "Complete") and from every Stage 4 sidebar. No certificate or badge.

## 13. Changing the Map

### 13.1 Three layers and three map states

| Layer | Holds | Changed by |
|---|---|---|
| Records | 4.1 Work Clues; 4.2 experiences, tasks, evidence; 4.3 reflections and choice | Their own module only |
| Current map | Directions on the map, next steps, "still exploring", investigation step, "where I am now" | 4.4 only |
| Stage 5 practice | Not built yet | Outside Stage 4 |

The map is always in exactly one state:
- **Empty** — no directions, not still exploring (before the learner chooses);
- **Directions** — one or two directions, each with its own next-step text (possibly empty);
- **Still exploring** — no directions; the investigation step is shown.

The investigation-step text is stored separately from the map state, so it can be kept while the map is in the Directions state (§13.3).

### 13.2 Rules that always apply
1. No 4.4 action writes to or deletes a 4.1, 4.2 or 4.3 record (D7). Required as a tested rule.
2. Written text is never discarded without the learner confirming (D10, D21).
3. Cancel on any confirmation leaves everything exactly as it was.
4. No map history (D8). Earlier thinking about a direction survives in its 4.3 reflection.

### 13.3 Every transition

| From | Learner action | Written text involved | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Empty | Add a direction | None | Direction added with an empty step. |
| Empty | Choose still exploring | Investigation text kept from earlier, if any | State becomes still exploring; any kept investigation text reappears in its field. |
| Directions | Add a second direction | None | Added. |
| Directions | Remove a direction **with** a written step | Its next step | **Confirm.** DRAFT copy: "This will remove the next step you wrote for *[direction]* from your map. Your Module 4.3 reflection stays as it is. Remove it?" OK: direction and its step removed. Cancel: nothing changes. |
| Directions | Remove a direction **without** a written step | None | Removed. No confirmation. |
| Directions | Choose still exploring, **any** step written | All written next steps | **Confirm.** DRAFT copy: "Choosing to keep exploring will remove your directions and the next steps you wrote for them from your map. Your Module 4.3 reflections stay as they are. Continue?" OK: directions and their steps removed; state becomes still exploring; kept investigation text reappears. Cancel: nothing changes. |
| Directions | Choose still exploring, **no** step written | None | Directions removed; state becomes still exploring. No confirmation. |
| Still exploring | Add a direction | Investigation step | **Kept, not discarded (D22).** State becomes Directions. The investigation text is not shown on the map, not printed, and does not count toward completion. Screen 3 shows: DRAFT copy "Your step for deciding is kept. It will come back if you choose to keep exploring again." No confirmation, because nothing is lost. |
| Still exploring | Edit or empty the investigation field | Investigation step | The learner's own edit. Emptying the field on purpose is the only way 4.4 removes it. |
| Still exploring | Unselect "still exploring" without adding a direction | Investigation step | State becomes Empty. The investigation text is kept, exactly as when a direction is added. |

Removing the last direction (either way above) leaves the map Empty. Any kept investigation text is untouched.

**Next steps are not kept after a confirmed removal.** A direction's step belongs to that direction on the map. Keeping hidden steps for removed directions would leave text the learner cannot see or reach. Re-adding a removed direction starts with an empty step.

**[requires specification]** If the specification finds that keeping the investigation step cannot be done clearly (for example in Export/Restore or the Restore confirmation), D21 applies instead: confirm before discarding it when a direction is added.

### 13.4 Walkthrough
A learner saves a map with direction A and a next step. Later, perhaps after Stage 5 practice, they prefer B.
1. They remove A on Screen 2 and confirm. A's next step leaves the map; A's 4.3 reflection and any 4.1 reaction to A are untouched.
2. If B has no saved 4.3 reflection, they examine it in 4.3 first. That creates a new 4.3 record, which is the right place for it.
3. They add B, write its next step, save, and print a new copy if they want one. Every Work Clue, task and 4.3 reflection is unchanged.

If the learner later changes their selection in 4.3, the map does not change (D2). The two may differ; the provisional reminder makes clear the map shows current thinking.

### 13.5 A map direction whose 4.3 reflection is no longer saved
The map's own choices never change because of edits elsewhere (§4). If a direction on the map later has no saved 4.3 reflection (source evidence: the live 4.3 page offers the judgment only as radio buttons that always set a value, and never clears a saved time — `public/shared/m43.js`, judgment `change` handler and `saveReflection`; so this arises only from restored or altered saved data, for example a backup whose judgment value is invalid and is dropped by `normalizeExploration` in `public/shared/state.js`):
- it stays on the map with its next step;
- the map shows "No judgment saved in Module 4.3" and whatever 4.3 content exists;
- it can still be removed (with the usual confirmation if a step is written);
- **[requires specification]** whether it can be re-added after removal follows D3 (only with a saved reflection).

## 18. UX, Accessibility and AI-Slop Filter
